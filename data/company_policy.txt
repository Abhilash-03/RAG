Company Leave Policy

Employees receive 24 paid leaves per year.
Employees must inform their manager at least two days before taking planned leave.
Employees should submit leave requests through the employee portal.
Unplanned absences must be communicated to the manager as soon as possible.

Remote Work Policy

Employees can work remotely up to three days per week.
Remote work requires manager approval.
Employees in their probation period are not eligible for remote work.
Remote employees must remain available during normal working hours.

Working Hours

The company's working hours are from 10 AM to 7 PM.
Employees are expected to attend all required meetings.
